02 / FIT CHECKLIST

What should a good fit feel like?

  • Band sits level and feels secure without digging in.
  • Straps stay in place without carrying all the support.
  • Fabric lies smoothly without pinching or gaping.
  • Move, sit and breathe naturally before deciding.
  • Try another size if comfort or coverage feels wrong.
Measurements and sizing vary by brand, design and region. Use the official label chart before buying.
